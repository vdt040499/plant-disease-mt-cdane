Overview of the proposed framework. (a) Field-adaptive Background Recomposition (FBR): SAM segments the laboratory leaf from a center point prompt, the mask is refined morphologically, and the leaf is alpha-composited onto a real-field background. (b) CDAN+E + Mean Teacher: the student (feature extractor Φ, label classifier G) is trained with the classification loss L_cls; the domain discriminator D receives the multilinear map f⊗g with entropy conditioning through a Gradient Reversal Layer (GRL) and gives the adversarial loss L_cdan; the EMA teacher provides the consistency loss L_cons on target images. Solid arrows: forward pass; dashed red arrows: backpropagation; dashed orange arrow: EMA update.
